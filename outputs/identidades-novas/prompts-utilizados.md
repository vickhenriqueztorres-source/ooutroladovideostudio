```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: professional YouTube channel visual identity presentation board, landscape 16:9
Primary request: create an original, premium, non-institutional visual identity for a Brazilian faceless documentary channel named "BRECHA", about scams, manipulation, AI fraud and invisible digital systems.
Scene/backdrop: cinematic near-black and warm off-white editorial background, with realistic everyday objects and interfaces cut by one precise luminous coral-orange fissure revealing a hidden layer.
Subject: a cohesive board showing one strong avatar symbol, the exact wordmark "BRECHA", a banner concept, two representative YouTube thumbnail compositions, and one motion-graphics frame language.
Style/medium: sophisticated editorial design mixed with cinematic 3D and restrained photorealism; contemporary media brand, curious and tense, not a cybersecurity company.
Composition/framing: clean landscape presentation board with five clearly separated visual zones, generous negative space, immediately comparable.
Lighting/mood: controlled dramatic light, premium documentary tension.
Color palette: near-black, bone white, charcoal, one signature coral-orange fissure.
Text (verbatim): "BRECHA"
Constraints: render BRECHA exactly once as the only readable word; original design; strong silhouette; production-ready art direction; no extra text; no watermark.
Avoid: shield, padlock, hacker hoodie, binary code, blue corporate palette, bank branding, antivirus UI, news broadcast graphics, generic cyberpunk neon, visual clutter.
```
